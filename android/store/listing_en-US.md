## Short description
View PDFs, highlight and annotate, organize pages, then save and share.

## Full description
HanjangPDF lets you view, mark up and organize PDF documents on your phone or tablet.

■ View
- Vertical scrolling, pinch to zoom, fit to width
- Find text (next/previous, matches highlighted)
- Select and copy text

■ Mark up
- Highlighter (yellow, green, pink), sticky notes, text boxes (size, alignment, color), rectangles (stroke and fill color, width, opacity)
- Eraser, undo and redo
- Saved the same way as HanjangPDF for PC, so marks added on one can be edited or removed on the other

■ Organize pages
- Long-press and drag thumbnails to reorder
- Rotate, delete or export selected pages; insert another PDF

■ Save and share
- 'Save as' a new file by default (your original stays untouched). Overwriting the original happens only when you choose it, and the original is restored if the save fails
- 'Share' sends a copy to the app you choose

■ Privacy
- No internet permission: the app never sends your documents anywhere by itself
- No storage permission: it opens only files you pick
- No ads, no analytics, no sign-up

Korean and English interface (follows the device language), with TalkBack and keyboard support.
HanjangPDF is free software (GNU AGPL v3) and uses the MuPDF engine.
Source: https://github.com/hayden90/hanjang-pdf
