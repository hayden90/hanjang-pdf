# Microsoft Store listing (en-US) draft

## Description
HanjangPDF is a PDF viewer for Windows, made for reading documents comfortably.

- View: open several documents in tabs, find text, zoom, fit width or page, print
- Page editing: drag pages to reorder, insert another PDF, delete, rotate, export selected pages
- Markup: highlights, notes, text boxes and rectangles, eraser, Ctrl+Z to undo
- Table cell selection: drag to select and copy text cell by cell in tables
- Text recognition: makes scanned or printed pages (text stored as images) selectable, copyable and searchable using the OCR built into Windows. Recognition runs on your PC only.
- Compare two versions side by side

The interface is available in English and Korean.
Your documents and usage history are never sent anywhere.
HanjangPDF is free software (GNU AGPL v3). Source: https://github.com/hayden90/hanjang-pdf

## Product features
Open several documents in tabs
Reorder, insert, delete and rotate pages
Highlights, notes, text boxes and rectangles
Select and copy text by table cell
OCR for scanned pages (on your PC only)
Compare two versions side by side
Markup prints with the document
